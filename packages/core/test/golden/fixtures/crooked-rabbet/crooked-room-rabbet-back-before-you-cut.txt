Crooked room, rabbet back — before you cut
==========================================

Read this before loading the first file. None of it is in the DXF files themselves.

1. Sheet 1 is cut in 3 tiles. Drill the TILE_REG holes through into the spoilboard, pin them, cut the tile, then slide the stock 980 mm along X against the fence and re-pin.

2. Sheet 1: C1-B-SIDE-L, C1-B-SIDE-R cross a tile seam, so each tile carries only its own stretch of the profile, on OUTLINE_OPEN (THROUGH_OPEN for a cut-out). Cut an outline with the cutter on the right of the direction of travel and a cut-out with it on the left, and let each stop at the seam — closing it there would cut the part in two.
